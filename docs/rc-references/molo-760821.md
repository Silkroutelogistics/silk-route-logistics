## Pickup / Delivery Instructions (verbatim — printed IDENTICALLY at both stops)

> \*\*\*MacroPoint required\*\*\* Failure to accept and stay compliant with geotracking for the duration of the shipment will result in a **$150 fine**. If load is relayed to a second driver, follow up is needed and tracking must be sent again. **If load cannot be tracked by carrier, that carrier cannot haul this load.** Trailer must be food grade, clean, dry, odor free, no holes or debris and must be swept out. All loads require at least 2 load locks and straps that must be adjusted after every stop to ensure product does not shift in transit. \*\*\*DETENTION REQUIREMENTS\*\*\* All driver detention requests MUST BE submitted to Molo **within 30 calendar days** of detention occurrence in order for reimbursement to be considered. Driver is responsible for providing signed paperwork with in and out times listed. Driver must double check paperwork prior to leaving shipper to confirm delivery destination and order. PLEASE NOTE: It is the carrier's responsibility to ensure seals are in place on all outbound loads and the seal # is listed on the BOL. **On multi-stop loads, the seal will remain intact until first delivery and driver is responsible to then seal with a padlock to all further destinations.** If seal needs to be broken upon arrival to destination the carrier needs to note the reason and time on the BOL, reseal the trailer with a new seal or padlock and note the new seal # on the BOL.

The full block is duplicated verbatim under both the pickup and the delivery —
the same TMS de-duplication defect Schneider exhibits with facility notes.

Two provisions stand out:

- **Tracking is a gating condition, not just a fined duty**: "If load cannot be
  tracked by carrier, that carrier cannot haul this load." Compare GlobalTranz
  the following year: "voluntary and not required."
- **Padlock-after-first-stop** is the only multi-stop seal-continuity procedure
  in the corpus. It solves a real problem (a seal cannot survive the first
  delivery) that no other document addresses.

## Temperature-controlled guidelines (page 2, verbatim)

> If this is a Temperature Controlled Shipment Please Follow These Guidelines:
> 1. Run all reefers on **Continuous** unless specific written instructions are given to do otherwise
> 2. Run reefer at the temperature on BOL's. If no temperature on BOL's please call in (847) 306-3557 for instructions
> 3. Pursuant to the carrier contract of DJM Logistics, LLC, dba MoLo Solutions, carrier will provide an amount of cargo insurance coverage sufficient to cover the loss or damage of any commodities and cargo carried. Carrier's cargo insurance policy must not exclude from coverage any commodities or cargo carried on this order. If carrier's cargo insurance policy contains a schedule of covered vehicles, carrier will not transport any cargo on this shipment using a vehicle that is not listed as a scheduled vehicle on carrier's cargo insurance policy. Carrier agrees that in transportation the shipment described above, it will comply with all U.S. DOT regulations applicable to its operations while transporting said shipment, including but not limited to, drivers' hours of service.

The block is **conditional in prose but unconditional in rendering** — it prints
on this dry load with an "If this is..." preamble. Item 3 is an insurance
covenant misfiled under a reefer heading, which is a numbering error in the
original.

The reefer default is **Continuous unless written instructions say otherwise**,
and the temperature authority is **the BOL**, not the confirmation — the inverse
of GlobalTranz, which makes the driver verify the BOL matches the confirmation.

## Structural observations

- **The shared-template evidence is the headline finding.** MoLo and
  C.H. Robinson carry the same acceptance clause and the same exclusive-use
  clause, typo included. Any SRL claim to have "matched industry standard"
  language should be read against this: much of the industry is not converging
  on a standard, it is propagating one document.
- **Two different deadlines for overlapping paperwork in the same document**
  (24 hours for accessorial receipts, 30 days for detention requests). Free-text
  terms scattered across stop blocks and footer blocks cannot be kept
  consistent — the argument for SRL rendering deadlines from a single field.
- **Geotracking as a hard gate** ("cannot haul this load") plus a $150 fine for
  dropping compliance mid-load. MoLo prices the failure; NTG pays for the
  acceptance; GlobalTranz makes it voluntary. Three postures within 12 months.
- **Padlock-after-first-delivery** is a genuinely useful multi-stop seal
  procedure worth borrowing if SRL ever runs multi-stop reefer freight.
- Reefer default is **Continuous**, matching Scotlynn's "Continuous Required".
  Two of the corpus's cold-chain documents default to continuous run mode; SRL's
  `TEMPERATURE CONTROL` block should not default to cycle.
