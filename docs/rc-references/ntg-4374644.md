# Nolan Transportation Group (NTG) — Carrier Confirmation, NTG Load # 4374644

Retrieved 2026-08-14 from
`https://www.grouprac.com/Documents/4374644Rate%20Confirmation.pdf`
(claims-recovery host; the carrier's own copy). Live TMS output, 2 pages
(page 2 is a carrier-portal advertisement). Load dated 7/21/2021 – 7/22/2021,
Buffalo NY → Moonachie NJ, FAK, 35,000 lbs, total $1,425.00.
Carrier R A C Transport LLC (MC# 921929). Individual contact details redacted;
company names and MC# retained (public FMCSA data).

**The finding here is the rate table.** NTG does not pay a flat linehaul. It
pays for compliance behaviours as separate, named line items.

---

## Contact structure (page 1)

Three parallel columns, each with P: / E: fields:

> ACCOUNT MANAGER | CARRIER SALES REP | NTG TRACKING & AFTER HOURS

A dedicated tracking-and-after-hours column alongside the two commercial
contacts — the only document in the corpus that treats tracking as its own
contact channel rather than a duty routed to the booking rep.

## CHARGES (page 1, verbatim)

> CHARGES:
> $1,225.00 LINE HAUL
> **$50.00 MACROPOINT ACCEPTED - MACRO POINT**
> **$150.00 POD UPON DELIVERY - POD UPON DELIVERY**
> $1,425.00 TOTAL

$200 of a $1,425 load — **14% of total pay** — is contingent on two compliance
acts: accepting geotracking, and returning the POD promptly. These are not
accessorials and not bonuses; they are printed as line items in the rate itself,
so the headline rate the carrier agreed to already assumes compliance.

The penalties elsewhere in the document mirror the same two amounts exactly:

> POD MUST BE SIGNED AND SENT BACK ASAP AFTER DELIVERY OR SUBJECT TO $150 RATE REDUCTION!

> 200.00 WILL BE DEDUCTED FROM RATE IF POD IS NOT IS NOT RECEIVED WITHIN 48 HOURS OF SCHEDULED DELIVERY, OR IF THIS RATE CONFIRMATION IS USED AS A POD!

So the same behaviour is framed twice — once as something you are paid for, once
as something you are docked for. ($150 vs $200 for what reads as the same POD
failure is an internal inconsistency in the document; "IS NOT IS NOT" is a typo
in the original.)

Three provisions here are unique in the corpus:

- **"ALL FREIGHT TRAILERS MUST BE 10 YEARS OR NEWER"** — an equipment-age
  requirement. No other document sets one.
- **"NTG DOES NOT ADVANCE FUNDS FOR ANY REASON"** — a flat refusal where every
  other broker prices advances (Allen Lund 2%/$25, C.H. Robinson 3%/$15,
  J.B. Hunt flat $10).
- **Amendment must be by a newly issued rate confirmation**, not by note,
  email or annotation. Accessorial approval is a document-issuance event.

## Double-brokering (page 1, verbatim)

> Double Brokering this load will cause this contract to become Null & Void. Double Brokering is strictly prohibited!

Note the remedy: the contract is voided. Compare Allen Lund (pay the delivering
carrier direct + up to $5,000 investigation charge), Schneider (voids the
obligation to pay), Echo (compensation may be withheld). NTG's voiding
construction is the weakest of the four — a void contract is an awkward basis
for withholding money already earned.

## Structural observations

- **Compliance is priced into the linehaul, not bolted on as accessorials.**
  This is a genuinely different model from every other document in the corpus
  and the closest analogue to what SRL's Compass Score is trying to do — except
  NTG settles it in cash on the load rather than in a score over time. Worth
  noting that NTG pays $50 for the *acceptance* of tracking, not for maintained
  compliance; MoLo fines $150 for failing to *stay* compliant. Same lever, two
  directions.
- **The document contradicts itself on the POD penalty** ($150 in the delivery
  block, $200 in the footer). A carrier reading it cannot tell its exposure.
  This is the practical cost of putting the same rule in two places in free
  text — the exact failure mode SRL avoided by making the 24-hour deadline a
  single rendered field.
- **Page 2 is advertising.** NTG spends half the document selling a portal. That
  is a real choice about what a rate confirmation is for, and it is the opposite
  of Scotlynn's two-page austerity.
- Equipment age, no-advances, and amendment-by-reissue are three enforceable,
  cheap policies that cost nothing to state and are absent from most of the
  corpus.
