## Structural observations

- **Check-call cadence is specified with a clock time and a tolerance**:
  arrival/departure within 30 minutes of occurrence, plus one call per day
  *prior to 10:00am*. This is the most precisely specified tracking obligation
  in the corpus, and it is attributed to the *customer*, not to C.H. Robinson.
- **Exclusive-use is a liquidated-damages forfeiture**, not a fine. The drafting
  is careful — "not as penalty, but as liquidated damages" is the language that
  survives a penalty-clause challenge. Identical wording appears in the MoLo
  document, which suggests a common industry template ancestor.
- **The fuel surcharge is disclosed but not itemised.** SRL's Commitment #2
  ("FSC itemized on every rate confirmation") is a genuinely stronger position
  than the largest broker in the market takes. Worth knowing that the claim is
  differentiating rather than table stakes.
- **Rate reduction for lateness is asserted without a number.** "Rate may be
  reduced" with no schedule — the discretion is unbounded. Compare GlobalTranz
  (up to 20% per day) and NTG ($200 flat), which at least publish the exposure.
- No detention rate, no free-time definition, no TONU term anywhere in the
  document. Detention is entirely "authorized and approved prior to or at time
  of occurrence."
