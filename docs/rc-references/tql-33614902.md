**Detention free time is differentiated by appointment type**: 3 hours free on an
appointment load, 4 hours free on FCFS. That is the cleanest answer in the
corpus to the question SRL's README records as still open — *"the document says
'2 hrs free' without saying free from what."* TQL's answer: free time runs from
the appointment when there is one, and is longer when there isn't, because
there is no appointment to measure from.

$25/hr detention is the lowest rate in the corpus (J.B. Hunt $40–50,
Scotlynn $50). $250 layover matches J.B. Hunt's automated rate and Echo's.

## Carrier Requirements (page 4, verbatim)

> **Driver(s) MUST accept and maintain tracking services throughout transit. Failure to accept TQL provided tracking within a timely manner will result in removal from the shipment prior to dispatch for non-compliance.**
> One of the following tracking systems must be accepted and maintained throughout transit:
> · TQL Carrier Dashboard tracking
> · Project 44
> · MacroPoint
> Failure to maintain tracking throughout transit may result in damages/fees being assessed for non-compliance.

> **All loads must be run on company owned equipment. IF carrier is utilizing leased or rented equipment this must be disclosed prior to accepting the carrier rate confirmation.**
> · Upon disclosure of rented or leased equipment the carrier MUST provide lease agreements, a list of scheduled autos, truck/trailer identification numbers as well as emergency contact information of the leasing party.
> · Failure to disclose this information within a timely manner will result in removal from the shipment prior to dispatch.

> All trailers MUST be food grade compliant: clean, dry, no holes, leaks, odors, and free of debris.
> **Upon arrival to shipper, if a trailer is deemed non-compliant carriers will not be issued a TONU compensation for detention due to cleaning efforts.**

> Driver(s) are responsible for notifying TQL immediately of any irregularities in the loading/unloading process (inaccurate BOLs, Counts, Commodities, OS&D, etc.).

> Driver(s) MUST ensure that the load is properly secured for transit. IF the shipper applies a seal at loading the seal # must be recorded on the BOLs and remain intact throughout transit.

> **If driver/carrier fails to note reefer BOLs with requested info prior to arriving to delivery, this can result in delayed payment and possibly non-payment of freight charges.**

The **leased/rented equipment disclosure rule** is the strongest anti-fraud
provision in the corpus. Requiring lease agreements, scheduled autos, unit
numbers and the lessor's emergency contact *before* dispatch attacks the exact
mechanism double-brokers and cargo thieves use — showing up with equipment that
does not belong to the MC on the confirmation.

> BOLs/PODs must be submitted to TQL **within 24-hours of delivery.** Failure to provide PODs within this time frame may result in delayed payments or forfeiture of requested detention/accessorials.
> **In/Out times must be listed on the BOL, legible, and signed off by the shipper/receiver for detention eligibility.**
> Do not pay for lumpers at the receiver. You are not to be charged any lumpers. Call TQL if the receiver is attempting to make you pay for a lumper. If payment is made for a lumper, you are liable for payment and will not be reimbursed.

> Late delivery may result in non-payment of freight charges, and **special damages as a consequence of being late may apply. This includes, but is not limited to, freight charges for expedited shipments, packaging materials, additional labor charges, storage charges, spoiled product, loss of sale, the expense of any additional equipment, service, or alternate transportation arrangements** that need to be utilized as a result of late delivery.

The special-damages enumeration is the broadest consequential-loss exposure in
the corpus, and it expressly includes **loss of sale** — a pure economic loss
that Carmack would not normally reach.

## Structural observations

- **Detention free time keyed to appointment type (3h appt / 4h FCFS)** is the
  single most directly reusable finding for SRL's open "free from what" question.
- **Tracking is a payment precondition, stated twice**: no detention without the
  tracker on for the whole load, and removal from the load for failing to accept
  tracking before dispatch. TQL, MoLo and NTG all monetise tracking compliance;
  only TQL makes it a dispatch gate *and* a detention gate.
- **The equipment-provenance disclosure rule** is worth serious consideration for
  SRL. Compass Engine already screens the carrier; this screens the *equipment
  actually showing up*, which is where identity substitution happens.
- **The document contradicts itself on free time** (page-1 note "4hrs free" for
  FCFS vs page-2 terms "does not begin for at least 3 hours"). Third instance in
  this corpus of the same failure mode — free-text operating terms drifting from
  structured terms within one document.
- Fuel is bundled into a single `Line Haul + Fuel` row with no disclosed split.
  Against this, SRL's itemised-FSC commitment is a real differentiator, not a
  parity feature.
