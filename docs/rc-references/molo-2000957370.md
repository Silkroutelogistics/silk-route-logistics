> Run all reefers on continuous unless specific written instructions are given to do otherwise. Run reefer at the temperature on BOL. If no temperature on BOL, please call +1 (847) 306-3557 for instructions.

This is a design choice worth noting: rather than conditionally rendering a
temperature block only on reefer loads, MoLo prints a self-qualifying paragraph
on every document. It cannot be wrong, and it cannot be silently omitted by a
data gap.

## Why this document matters to SRL

- **Acceptance by silence.** MoLo's model is not acceptance-by-conduct: terms
  bind unless the carrier objects **within 24 hours of receipt or before work
  begins, whichever is earlier**. That is a materially different mechanism from
  SRL's, and it puts the burden of objection on the carrier with a clock on it.
- **24-hour accessorial and lumper receipt deadline** — independent corroboration
  of the 24-hour paperwork window SRL ratified, from a different broker than
  Leonard's Express.
- **Reseal after each stop** — SRL's seal line says record it and let the
  receiver remove it; it says nothing about resealing on a multi-stop run.
- **Exclusive use with forfeiture of the entire freight bill as liquidated
  damages.** Covenant. Steam Logistics carries the same requirement without the
  forfeiture remedy.
- **FSMA named explicitly**, and food-grade trailer tied to it. SRL hauls
  refrigerated CPG for a food shipper and names neither.
- **Scheduled-vehicle cargo insurance exclusion** — now 4 of 7 documents.
- The temperature-guidance paragraph printing unconditionally is the cheapest
  possible fix for the failure mode where a reefer load renders no temperature
  because the data was never captured.
