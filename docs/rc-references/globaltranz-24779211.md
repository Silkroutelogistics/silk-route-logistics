## Structural observations

- **Two independent graduated penalties, both expressed as a rate against time.**
  Service failure: up to 20% per day. Paperwork lateness: 1% per day after a
  5-day POD deadline. This is the only document in the corpus that prices
  document lateness on a sliding scale rather than as a flat deduction or a hard
  forfeiture. It is a materially fairer construction than NTG's flat $200 or
  Allen Lund's silence, and worth weighing against SRL's 24-hour rule, which is
  currently a cliff.
- **A mechanical-failure carve-out.** "Mechanical failure delay with supporting
  receipt will not penalized" *[sic]* is the only excuse provision in the entire
  corpus. Every other broker's lateness penalty is strict liability.
- **The FSMA covenant is on a dry-van load of plastic articles.** GlobalTranz
  prints the full four-statute food-safety chain on freight that has nothing to
  do with food. That is the low-cost way to carry the covenant: put it in the
  boilerplate and let it bind whenever it happens to be relevant. Directly
  relevant to the "Reefer FSMA continuous-temperature covenant" item SRL has
  parked as a counsel decision — this shows the covenant does not require a
  reefer-conditional block to exist.
- **Precedence is stated in an unusual two-step**: the master Agreement governs,
  "and then" the rate confirmation's terms apply. Read literally that is a
  gap-filling order rather than a conflict rule — the Agreement wins, and the
  rate confirmation supplies whatever the Agreement is silent on.
- **Binding without signature is explicit**, and the document still prints a
  carrier signature line. The signature is invited, not required.
- Temperature responsibility is pushed entirely to the carrier and tied to a
  verification act at pickup — the driver must confirm the BOL temperature
  matches the confirmation. That is a cheap, checkable control that costs the
  broker nothing and is absent from SRL's document.
