## Why this document matters to SRL

- **Pay-when-paid, stated three separate ways** — on accessorials generally, on
  detention/TONU/damage specifically, and on the whole freight bill when the
  customer refuses over paperwork. Earlier SRL research explicitly recommended
  AGAINST adopting pay-when-paid, because broker-fault TONU triggers (wrong
  pickup number, wrong address, double-booking) are never shipper-reimbursable
  and the clause would leave the carrier unpaid for the broker's own error.
  Greatwide is the proof that real brokers do it anyway. It is a covenant, and
  it is Dirk's call, not an engineering one.
- **180-day outer paperwork limit**, versus TIS 30 days, Schneider 90 days,
  MoLo 24 hours for accessorials, Scotlynn end-of-day. Enormous variance — there
  is no industry standard, which means SRL's 24 hours is a policy choice it owns
  rather than a norm it follows.
- **An explicit return channel for the signed confirmation.** SRL prints a
  signature block and never says where to send it.
- The broker prints **its own MC number** in the bill-to block.
- One page. Proof that length is a choice, not a requirement.
