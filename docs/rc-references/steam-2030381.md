## Why this document matters to SRL

- **"Motor carrier and driver information must match the details provided on
  this Rate Confirmation. NO DOUBLE BROKERING."** This is the single most
  useful sentence in the corpus, because it supplies the *reason* for capturing
  driver and unit details. SRL added a driver/cell/truck/trailer capture line in
  v3.8.arm as a dispatch convenience. Steam turns the same data into a
  double-broker control: the details on the document are what must show up at
  the gate, and a mismatch is the tell. One sentence converts an administrative
  field into an anti-fraud device.
- **Two pages, ten lines of terms.** The most recent document in the corpus is
  also among the leanest. Length is not what makes a rate confirmation strong.
- **"All In Rate"** as a rate-table label rather than a paragraph.
- **Appointment Required + Shipping/Receiving Hours** as distinct fields.
- **Invoice must match the final rate confirmation**, with an instruction to
  call before invoicing a figure the carrier disputes. That prevents the
  invoice-mismatch dispute rather than adjudicating it afterward.
- **Relay Payments named on the rate confirmation itself** — relevant to SRL's
  decision not to open a money-code account, since it shows how brokers surface
  a payment rail to carriers who already use one.
- Exclusive use appears here **without** MoLo's forfeiture remedy — evidence
  that the covenant and its remedy are separable choices.
