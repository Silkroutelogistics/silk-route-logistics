# Broker-Carrier Agreement
*FOUNDATION EDITION · 1 SEPTEMBER 2026*
*Silk Route Logistics Inc. · MC# 1794414 · USDOT# 4526880 · 2317 S 35th St, Galesburg, Michigan 49053 · Reference BCA-2026-09-01-F10*

THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into by and between ________________________________ ("CARRIER") and Silk Route Logistics, Inc., a Michigan corporation ("BROKER"), effective as of the date of the last signature in the execution section at the end of this Agreement (the "Effective Date").
WHEREAS, BROKER is licensed by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration as a property broker under 49 U.S.C. sections 13904 and 13906, MC# 1794414, USDOT# 4526880; and
WHEREAS, CARRIER is registered with the FMCSA as a motor carrier with authority to operate in interstate, intrastate and/or foreign commerce and is qualified to transport freight as required by BROKER; and
WHEREAS, BROKER wishes to engage CARRIER to perform transportation within the limits of CARRIER's operating authority, and CARRIER wishes to perform it;
NOW THEREFORE, intending to be legally bound, the parties agree as follows.


## 1. Term
This Agreement runs for one year from the Effective Date and renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives at least thirty (30) days' written notice of termination under paragraph 34. Either party may terminate for any reason or no reason on that notice.


## 2. Carrier qualification and compliance
CARRIER warrants that all equipment and personnel used meet and comply with all requirements of the U.S. Department of Transportation and every other federal, state or provincial agency with jurisdiction, including weight and dimension limits, the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations, Hours of Service rules, drug and alcohol testing under 49 C.F.R. Part 382, and hazardous materials regulations under 49 C.F.R. Parts 171-180.
Driver qualification. CARRIER shall qualify every driver used on BROKER's freight in accordance with 49 C.F.R. Part 391, shall maintain a complete driver qualification file for each such driver, and shall meet its query and reporting obligations under the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse at 49 C.F.R. Part 382, Subpart G. CARRIER shall not assign to BROKER's freight any driver who is prohibited from operating a commercial motor vehicle.
Equipment condition. All equipment used shall be roadworthy, in good working order, maintained and inspected in accordance with 49 C.F.R. Part 396, suitable for the commodity tendered, and free of any condition that would render the freight unsafe, contaminated or unmerchantable.
CARRIER shall notify BROKER in writing immediately, and no later than twenty-four (24) hours, of any change in its safety rating, and shall provide BROKER copies of any FMCSA Notice of Changes or Notice of Claim relating to that change.
Cross-border operations. Where a shipment moves between the United States and Canada, CARRIER warrants that it holds the operating authority required in each jurisdiction, including any applicable provincial authority, CVOR or National Safety Code registration and a valid Canadian Safety Fitness Certificate, and that it complies with the requirements of U.S. Customs and Border Protection and the Canada Border Services Agency, including electronic manifest filing. CARRIER shall not break a customs seal or deviate from a bonded routing without authority. Where CARRIER holds C-TPAT or Partners in Protection status, it shall maintain that status and notify BROKER of any change.


## 3. Continuing qualification is a condition of each tender
CARRIER's qualification is continuing, not a one-time check at signing. As a condition of BROKER tendering any load, CARRIER must at that moment hold:
(a) active operating authority and all insurance required by paragraph 12;
(b) a safety rating that is Satisfactory or unrated, and in no event Conditional or Unsatisfactory;
(c) CSA BASIC scores below FMCSA intervention thresholds;
(d) no out-of-service order; and
(e) interstate operating authority that has been active and in good standing for not less than eighteen (18) months.
Where CARRIER's authority has been active at least twelve (12) months but fewer than eighteen (18), BROKER may tender only on a documented exception approved under BROKER's carrier-vetting standards. BROKER does not tender to authority active fewer than twelve (12) months.
If CARRIER stops meeting any of these at any time, CARRIER shall not accept or transport any load, and BROKER may decline or suspend tenders or terminate under paragraph 34 without liability.


## 4. Sanctions and authority integrity
CARRIER warrants that neither CARRIER nor any of its owners, officers or drivers is (a) listed on the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals list or any other U.S. government denied, debarred or restricted party list, or (b) operating under an MC or USDOT number that has been reincarnated, revoked, suspended or fraudulently obtained. CARRIER shall notify BROKER immediately if this changes.


## 5. No minimum volume
No minimum volume of freight is contemplated. BROKER may tender to other carriers. CARRIER may haul for other brokers and shippers. Neither party owes the other exclusivity.


## 6. Transport without delay
CARRIER shall transport BROKER's shipments without delay and shall notify BROKER immediately of any likelihood of delay, or of any incident or circumstance that will prevent or delay delivery.


## 7. Delivery receipts and bills of lading
Each shipment shall be evidenced by a Uniform Straight Bill of Lading naming CARRIER as the transporting carrier. CARRIER's drivers shall sign their company's name and record the seal number of any sealed trailer on every bill of lading.
CARRIER shall obtain from the consignee a complete, signed delivery receipt for each shipment and shall notify BROKER immediately of any exception noted on any document.
Under no circumstances shall CARRIER prepare a freight document listing BROKER as "Carrier" or "Shipper." Documents shall name BROKER as "broker" and CARRIER as "carrier." If a document is wrongly worded the parties will treat it as if it named them correctly. CARRIER shall indemnify BROKER against loss arising from errors in the bill of lading, including the showing of BROKER as carrier or shipper.
Before signing any bill of lading, CARRIER's driver shall count the goods where applicable and note any overage, shortage or visible damage. Failure to note exceptions renders CARRIER liable for the shortage or damage.
Where a shipper preloads and seals a trailer and no CARRIER representative was present to verify count or stowage, the shipment moves on a shipper's load and count basis.


## 8. This Agreement governs
If there is a conflict between this Agreement and any transportation document, including a bill of lading or Rate Confirmation, this Agreement governs, except that where a Rate Confirmation states an accessorial rate, cap, condition or notice requirement, that statement governs that load.
Any schedule, tariff, rate, classification or rule published or maintained by CARRIER does not apply to any shipment under this Agreement unless specifically incorporated here in writing.


## 9. Carrier is responsible for the transportation
CARRIER is wholly responsible for performing the transportation and for all costs of it, including equipment, its maintenance, and the persons who operate it. As to BROKER, CARRIER is an independent contractor and is wholly responsible for the people it hires, employs or uses. At its sole cost CARRIER shall furnish all equipment, pay all expenses of operating it, maintain it in good repair and condition, and use only competent, able and properly licensed personnel.


## 10. No control; no agency
BROKER's exercise of its rights under this Agreement, including carrier vetting and identity verification, continuing safety monitoring under paragraph 3, tracking and check calls under paragraph 22, and the service standards in paragraph 29, is undertaken solely to confirm CARRIER's qualifications and to protect BROKER and its customers.
Those rights do not grant BROKER any control or right of control over the manner, means, methods, routing, scheduling, equipment or personnel by which CARRIER performs transportation. CARRIER at all times retains exclusive control over the operation of its equipment and the supervision of its drivers and is solely responsible for their safe operation. Nothing here creates an agency, employment, joint employment, joint venture or principal-agent relationship.
No coercion. Nothing in this Agreement, and no schedule, service standard, check call, chargeback or Compass Score measurement under it, shall be construed to require or pressure CARRIER or any driver to operate in violation of the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations, including the Hours of Service rules. CARRIER and its drivers may decline or discontinue any movement that cannot be performed lawfully and safely, and BROKER shall take no adverse action, including any Compass Score consequence, for that refusal. This paragraph is intended to conform to 49 C.F.R. section 390.6.


## 11. Indemnification, limited to CARRIER's own fault
CARRIER shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless BROKER, its shipper customers, and their officers, directors, employees and agents from all claims, liabilities, judgments, damages, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising out of or caused by CARRIER's acts, omissions, negligence or willful misconduct, or that of CARRIER's employees, drivers, owner-operators, agents or subcontractors.
CARRIER's obligations under this paragraph are proportionate to CARRIER's degree of fault. Nothing in this Agreement requires CARRIER to indemnify, defend or hold harmless any party against liability arising from that party's own negligence or intentional acts or omissions. This paragraph is written to conform to Michigan Compiled Laws § 479.21 and to the anti-indemnity statutes of every other state whose law may apply. Where fault is concurrent, each party bears its proportionate share.
BROKER shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless CARRIER against claims to the extent directly caused by BROKER's own negligence or willful misconduct.
CARRIER shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless BROKER and its shipper customers against claims brought by CARRIER's employees, drivers, owner-operators, agents or subcontractors asserting employment by, or seeking wages, benefits or workers' compensation from, BROKER or a shipper customer. To the extent necessary to give effect to this obligation, CARRIER waives any exclusive-remedy defense available under workers' compensation or similar occupational statutes.
Each of BROKER's shipper customers is an express third-party beneficiary of this paragraph and may enforce it directly.
This paragraph survives termination.


## 12. Insurance
Throughout the term CARRIER shall maintain, at its own expense, insurance from insurers rated not less than A- (Excellent), Financial Size Category VII or better by AM Best, and admitted in Michigan or eligible surplus lines insurers. Each insurer must comply with all applicable FMCSA regulations.
Minimum coverages:
(a) Commercial Automobile Liability, combined single limit not less than $1,000,000 each occurrence, covering all vehicles however owned or used by CARRIER, including hired and non-owned. For hazardous materials, limits as required by 49 C.F.R. § 387.9.
(b) Commercial General Liability, occurrence basis, not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate.
(c) All-risk broad form Motor Truck Cargo Legal Liability, not less than $100,000 each occurrence, including loading and unloading.
(d) Workers' Compensation at statutory limits, or a valid exemption certificate, plus Employer's Liability of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence, per employee, and in the aggregate.
The cargo policy shall contain no exclusion or restriction that would foreseeably preclude or reduce coverage for the commodities transported under this Agreement, including exclusions for unattended or unattached trailers, theft, refrigeration or mechanical breakdown, or lack of refrigeration fuel.
Where CARRIER hauls temperature-controlled freight, the cargo policy shall include refrigeration and mechanical breakdown coverage, and the refrigeration breakdown deductible shall be listed separately on the certificate of insurance. Where CARRIER operates leased or interchanged equipment, CARRIER shall carry Trailer Interchange and Non-Trucking Liability. Any deductible or self-insured retention over $10,000 on a required policy shall be disclosed to BROKER in writing before tender.


## 13. Certificates, notice and standing
Before accepting any shipment CARRIER shall furnish BROKER a certificate from its insurers showing the coverage is in place and properly maintained, with expiration dates, and providing that written notice of cancellation, non-renewal or material change will be given to BROKER at least thirty (30) days in advance, or ten (10) days where cancellation is for non-payment of premium.
BROKER and its shipper customers shall be named as Additional Insured on a primary and non-contributory basis on the automobile and general liability policies, with waiver of subrogation in favor of BROKER, and as Loss Payee on the cargo policy.
CARRIER shall notify BROKER within twenty-four (24) hours of any cancellation, non-renewal, lapse or material change. On that notice, or on BROKER's own discovery of a deficiency, BROKER may immediately suspend tenders and withhold payment until compliant coverage is restored.
If a shipper requires higher limits, CARRIER will comply on thirty (30) days' advance written notice and provide new certificates.
On CARRIER's written request, BROKER shall provide evidence of the surety bond or trust fund it maintains under 49 U.S.C. § 13906 and 49 C.F.R. § 387.307.
Insurance limits do not cap CARRIER's liability. Exclusions in CARRIER's policies do not reduce CARRIER's obligations under this Agreement.


## 14. Liability for loss, damage or delay
CARRIER is liable for loss of, damage to, or delay of cargo from the time CARRIER takes possession at origin, or the trailer is loaded, whichever is first and regardless of whether a bill of lading has been issued, until delivery to the consignee and signature of the delivery receipt. Liability is governed by the Carmack Amendment, 49 U.S.C. § 14706.
Loss or damage is measured as the greater of actual replacement cost or the cost to repair, less reasonable salvage value, plus direct costs of packaging, handling and re-shipment, and reasonable amounts for securement, cleanup, towing, disposal and inspection.
CARRIER is solely responsible for claims arising from reckless, dishonest or illegal acts of its employees or agents, and from CARRIER furnishing contaminated equipment.
CARRIER shall not accept any shipment with a declared value over $100,000 without first informing BROKER and obtaining authorization. Where BROKER or its customer requires a higher cargo limit for a load, the increased valuation will be stated in the Rate Confirmation or bill of lading, and CARRIER's acceptance of that load is CARRIER's agreement to carry cargo insurance to that amount.


## 15. Claims handling
CARRIER shall acknowledge each cargo claim in writing within thirty (30) days of receipt and shall pay, decline, or make a firm written settlement offer within one hundred twenty (120) days, consistent with 49 C.F.R. §§ 370.5 and 370.9. If a claim cannot be resolved in that period CARRIER shall report the status and reason at that point and every sixty (60) days after until it is resolved.
Claims must be filed, and suit begun, within the minimum periods of 49 U.S.C. § 14706(e): not less than nine (9) months to file, and two (2) years from disallowance to sue. Claims for concealed damage filed within nine (9) months of delivery are handled like any other claim.
CARRIER shall pay BROKER amounts due on any freight claim within thirty (30) days of being notified of the amount and furnished substantiating documentation, and authorizes BROKER to deduct those amounts from sums owed to CARRIER.


## 16. Cargo theft
On theft or pilferage CARRIER shall immediately notify BROKER and law enforcement, preserve all evidence, cooperate fully with any investigation, and not settle any theft claim without BROKER's written consent. CARRIER remains liable for the full value of stolen cargo. Carmack carrier defenses do not apply to claims arising from cargo theft.


## 17. Refused and rejected freight
If cargo is refused or rejected for any reason CARRIER shall notify BROKER immediately and keep possession until BROKER gives written disposition instructions. CARRIER shall not sell, auction, dispose of or abandon rejected cargo without BROKER's written consent.
Where CARRIER stores a shipment before completing delivery, the shipment is deemed still in transit and CARRIER's full cargo liability continues without reverting to warehouseman liability.
CARRIER waives all rights of salvage or resale, and any right to claim an offset for salvage value. Whether damaged cargo is salvageable, and what it is worth, is determined by BROKER's customer.


## 18. Security-sensitive and high-value freight
For loads BROKER designates as high-value or security-sensitive, which may include food, beverage, nutritional and consumer-packaged goods, CARRIER shall, unless BROKER agrees otherwise in writing:
(a) not leave a loaded trailer unattended except inside a secured, fenced and monitored facility;
(b) not drop and hook in unsecured locations;
(c) make no stops within the first two hundred (200) miles or four (4) hours from pickup, absent emergency;
(d) use high-security ISO 17712-compliant seals, and a secondary locking device where BROKER requires one;
(e) maintain active location reporting throughout transit; and
(f) use team drivers or pre-approved secured parking for required rest; and
(g) not transport property of any person other than BROKER's customer on the same equipment while loaded, without BROKER's prior written consent.
Failure to comply creates, on theft or loss, a presumption of CARRIER negligence, and CARRIER waives any Carmack defense to the extent of that non-compliance.


## 19. Temperature-controlled freight
For refrigerated or temperature-controlled shipments CARRIER shall:
(a) pre-cool the equipment to the required temperature before loading;
(b) maintain the temperature stated in the Rate Confirmation throughout transit, and note the cargo temperature at pickup on the bill of lading;
(c) refuse the load and notify BROKER immediately if the temperature at pickup is more than two (2) degrees outside the required range;
(d) perform manufacturer-recommended maintenance on the refrigeration unit and keep records of it;
(e) keep the unit sufficiently fueled;
(f) use trailers with functioning temperature monitoring capable of showing the temperature was held for the entire transit, and refrigeration equipment capable of producing a downloadable report; and
(g) keep temperature records for three (3) years and provide them to BROKER or its customer on request.
Where goods are tendered and a reasonable person would understand them to need temperature control, and CARRIER has not been given instructions, CARRIER shall ask for and obtain those instructions before loading. Where instructions conflict or are unclear, CARRIER shall resolve them before accepting the load.


## 20. Food safety
For shipments of human or animal food, CARRIER shall comply with the Food Safety Modernization Act and the Sanitary Transportation of Human and Animal Food rule, 21 C.F.R. Part 1, Subpart O.
CARRIER shall present a clean, dry, odor-free trailer, free of pests, residue and prior-load contamination, and shall provide a wash-out or sanitation certificate on request. CARRIER shall not carry food in a trailer previously used for hazardous waste, garbage, refuse, rodenticide or pesticide without documented decontamination.
CARRIER acknowledges that food carried outside the handling instructions given may be treated as adulterated under 21 U.S.C. § 342(i), that an adulterated shipment may be refused at destination, and that CARRIER bears the risk of that rejection where it results from a broken seal or a failure to follow handling instructions.
CARRIER shall indemnify BROKER and its customers against claims, penalties, recalls and fines arising from CARRIER's failure to comply with food safety regulation.


## 21. Seals
Where BROKER, the shipper or the consignee requires it, CARRIER shall secure the shipment with a serialized seal, record the number on the bill of lading at origin, verify the seal at destination, and notify BROKER immediately if a seal is broken, tampered with or missing.
Except where law enforcement requires it, CARRIER shall not break a seal without BROKER's consent. Where law enforcement breaks a seal, CARRIER shall record the agency, time, location, and the officer's name and badge number on the bill of lading, re-seal with a serialized seal, record the second number, and tell BROKER as soon as reasonably possible.
Failure to maintain seal integrity creates a rebuttable presumption that any shortage or damage happened while the shipment was in CARRIER's possession.


## 22. Tracking, check calls and driver contact
CARRIER shall provide location updates as reasonably requested, including pickup confirmation, in-transit updates and delivery confirmation with signed Proof of Delivery, and shall respond to check-call requests within thirty (30) minutes during business hours. CARRIER shall give BROKER contact information for the driver on each load.
Tracking may be by any mutually agreed method, including ELD integration, third-party tracking platforms, BROKER's carrier portal, driver-initiated location sharing, or manual check calls. BROKER will not require CARRIER to integrate any particular ELD platform as a condition of doing business.
Where CARRIER or its driver gives a mobile number for operational messaging, CARRIER consents and confirms it has the driver's consent to receive operational and tracking messages at that number. A recipient may opt out at any time by replying STOP, and BROKER will honor it. Opting out does not affect CARRIER's standing under this Agreement or its Compass Score.


## 23. Incident reporting
CARRIER shall notify BROKER within two (2) hours of any accident, cargo theft, hazardous material release, driver arrest or citation, inspection resulting in an out-of-service order, or any other event that may give rise to a claim. Failure to give timely notice is a material breach.


## 24. Rates, Rate Confirmations and accessorials
Rates and charges are only those stated on the individual Rate Confirmation or Load Confirmation Sheet accepted by CARRIER before each shipment. Rate Confirmations may be sent electronically, and CARRIER's electronic acceptance, or CARRIER's pickup of the shipment, constitutes binding acceptance. No verbal rate will be honored.
All accessorial charges must be pre-approved by BROKER in writing before being incurred. Unauthorized accessorial charges are not owed.
The following accessorial terms apply uniformly to every carrier and every equipment type. They do not vary by tier. Where a Rate Confirmation states a different accessorial rate, cap, condition or notice requirement for a specific load, that statement governs that load under paragraph 8.

## 25. Documentation and payment
CARRIER shall submit a clean signed bill of lading, Proof of Delivery, lumper receipts and any other required documents within twenty-four (24) hours of delivery. CARRIER acknowledges that this same twenty-four hour window is what BROKER measures document timeliness against under the Compass Score described in paragraph 30.
CARRIER shall submit its complete invoice within one hundred eighty (180) days of delivery or waive payment for that shipment. Claims for undercharge must be brought within one hundred eighty (180) days of BROKER's receipt of the original invoice.
BROKER's standard payment terms are tier-based under the Caravan Partner Program and are set out in Schedule A, which is incorporated into and forms part of this Agreement: Silver Net-30, Gold Net-21, Platinum Net-14, measured from BROKER's receipt of a clean, complete invoice with signed bill of lading and Proof of Delivery. Standard tier payment is free of charge and is always available. The applicable tier is stated on each Rate Confirmation.
Quick Pay is a limited pilot governed exclusively by BROKER's separately executed Caravan Quick Pay Agreement. CARRIER may request admission. BROKER may approve or decline, and may withdraw the pilot on notice. Reaching a tier does not by itself grant admission. Quick Pay fees, elections and mechanics are as stated in the Caravan Quick Pay Agreement and recorded on the affected load. BROKER will not deduct a Quick Pay fee unless a fee is recorded on the load, the Caravan Quick Pay Agreement is signed, and Quick Pay is enabled for CARRIER.
Payment may be delayed where paperwork is incomplete or illegible, or where there is an open claim, shortage, damage, rejection, temperature exception, late pickup or delivery, double brokering, or back-solicitation.


## 26. Offset
BROKER may offset amounts owed to CARRIER against amounts CARRIER owes BROKER, including cargo claims, chargebacks under paragraph 29, pallet charges, and amounts advanced by BROKER.


## 27. Exclusive billing and waiver of lien
BROKER has the exclusive right to bill the customer. CARRIER shall not pursue collection against any shipper, receiver, consignor or consignee unless BROKER authorizes it in writing. On CARRIER's receipt of payment from BROKER, any right CARRIER has to payment from a third party for those services is assigned to BROKER.
CARRIER shall not withhold delivery of any freight because of a dispute about charges. CARRIER waives and releases all liens it might otherwise have over goods of BROKER or its customer in CARRIER's possession. CARRIER is liable for costs BROKER incurs to secure release or delivery of cargo, including amounts paid to warehouses or towing companies.
CARRIER may seek payment from a shipper only if BROKER has failed to pay within the applicable term, CARRIER has given BROKER fifteen (15) business days' written notice of intent, and BROKER has neither paid nor given a reasonable explanation. CARRIER shall not pursue a shipper that can prove payment to BROKER.


## 28. Factoring and assignment of payment
CARRIER may assign its right to payment to a factoring company or other third party, provided CARRIER delivers a valid written Notice of Assignment to BROKER before BROKER remits. BROKER's payment in accordance with a valid Notice is payment to CARRIER and absolves BROKER of further liability for it. Where CARRIER gives multiple or conflicting Notices, BROKER's compliance with any one of them absolves BROKER as to that shipment.
No assignment, factoring, pledge or security interest modifies, limits or terminates BROKER's or its customer's rights of offset or recoupment, or any claim they hold against CARRIER. Those rights are preserved and are superior to the rights of any factor, assignee or creditor, regardless of any notice to the contrary. CARRIER shall notify any such party of this paragraph.
CARRIER shall indemnify BROKER against loss arising from any assignment or transfer of rights by CARRIER, and from any unverified change of remittance instructions.


## 29. Service standards and chargebacks
CARRIER shall pick up and deliver within the appointment times and delivery windows, including any Must-Arrive-By Date, stated on the Rate Confirmation, and shall tell BROKER immediately of any expected delay.
Where CARRIER's late pickup or delivery, missed appointment, short or non-compliant delivery, or documentation failure directly causes a customer chargeback, fine or penalty against BROKER or its customer, CARRIER is liable for the documented amount, provided BROKER documents the charge and its causal link. This does not apply to delays caused by BROKER, the shipper, the consignee, facility scheduling or force majeure, and, absent CARRIER's gross negligence or willful misconduct, shall not exceed the line-haul revenue of the affected load.


## 30. Performance measurement
CARRIER acknowledges that BROKER measures performance through its published seven-factor Compass Score, and that placement and advancement within the Caravan Partner Program are performance-based on criteria published at silkroutelogistics.ai/carriers. BROKER may change program criteria on thirty (30) days' notice. Every approved carrier begins at Silver from the first load.


## 31. Double brokering is prohibited
CARRIER shall transport all freight tendered by BROKER only on equipment operated under CARRIER's own authority. CARRIER shall not re-broker, co-broker, subcontract, assign, interline or transfer any shipment to any other person or entity without BROKER's prior written consent. This does not apply to a person leased to CARRIER under 49 C.F.R. Part 376.
On breach, CARRIER remains directly and fully liable to BROKER as if CARRIER had hauled the freight under its own authority, including cargo liability and all indemnity obligations.
Where BROKER discovers an unconsented re-brokering, BROKER may pay the actual performing carrier directly, and that payment discharges BROKER's payment obligation to CARRIER in full for that load. BROKER may also terminate immediately. CARRIER forfeits any right to payment for the affected shipment.
If CARRIER uses a third-party dispatch service, CARRIER shall disclose it in writing. A dispatcher is CARRIER's limited agent for load coordination only and has no authority to bind CARRIER's operating authority to another carrier, to re-broker, or to receive or redirect payment except under a valid Notice of Assignment under paragraph 28.


## 32. Identity verification and remittance security
As a condition of tender, BROKER may verify CARRIER's identity, authority, insurance, equipment and drivers through SAFER, the FMCSA registration system, third-party vetting platforms, and telephone callback to CARRIER's number of record. CARRIER shall not misrepresent its identity, authority, MC or USDOT number, or affiliation.
Each shipment shall be picked up by CARRIER's own verified driver operating CARRIER's equipment under CARRIER's authority. CARRIER shall not let any other person or entity take possession of BROKER's freight. Pickup by an unverified party is a material breach and CARRIER is fully liable for the resulting loss.
Any change to CARRIER's remittance instructions, banking details or factoring assignment must be in writing on CARRIER letterhead, signed by an authorized officer, and is not effective until BROKER verifies it by telephone callback to CARRIER's number of record. BROKER has no liability for payment made on instructions on file before a verified change.
CARRIER shall indemnify BROKER against loss arising from freight fraud, identity theft, fictitious pickup, double brokering or unauthorized payment redirection involving CARRIER's authority, credentials or systems.


## 33. Confidentiality and non-solicitation
Neither party shall disclose the terms of this Agreement to a third party without the other's written consent, except as required by law, to a parent, subsidiary or affiliate, or to a freight-bill auditor or professional adviser bound to equivalent confidentiality. BROKER may disclose paragraph 11 to a shipper customer for the purpose of that customer evaluating or asserting its rights as a third-party beneficiary under that paragraph.
CARRIER shall not disclose BROKER's customer identities, rates, lane data, volumes or business practices.
For twelve (12) months after the last load transported for BROKER, CARRIER shall not solicit or accept traffic, directly or indirectly, from any shipper, consignor, consignee or customer where the availability of that traffic first became known to CARRIER through BROKER's efforts, or where that customer's traffic was first tendered to CARRIER by BROKER.
If CARRIER breaches this paragraph, CARRIER shall pay BROKER liquidated damages of fifteen percent (15%) of the gross transportation revenue CARRIER receives from that customer during the twelve (12) months following the breach, and shall provide documentation BROKER requests to verify that revenue. The parties agree this is a reasonable pre-estimate of damages that would be difficult to calculate precisely.
Breach of this paragraph would cause irreparable harm, and BROKER may seek injunctive relief without proving actual damages or posting bond.


## 34. Termination
Either party may terminate on thirty (30) days' written notice, with or without cause.
Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within ten (10) days of written notice, becomes insolvent, files or suffers a bankruptcy petition not dismissed within thirty (30) days, or ceases operations. BROKER may terminate immediately if CARRIER loses its operating authority or required insurance, is placed under an out-of-service order, fails a condition of paragraph 3, re-brokers in violation of paragraph 31, or fails to give a valid Notice of Assignment.
Termination is forward-looking. Shipments already in CARRIER's possession or in transit when termination takes effect shall be completed by CARRIER, and BROKER shall pay for them on the terms that applied when they were tendered. Termination blocks future tenders only.
Obligations relating to payment, insurance, cargo liability, indemnity, confidentiality, non-solicitation, identity verification, chargebacks, records and dispute resolution survive termination.


## 35. Records and audit
CARRIER shall keep records relating to shipments under this Agreement, including bills of lading, Proofs of Delivery, temperature records, seal logs, tracking data, insurance certificates and driver qualification files, for not less than three (3) years, or longer where the law requires.
