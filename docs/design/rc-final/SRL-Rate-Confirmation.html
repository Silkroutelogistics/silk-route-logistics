Conditions

Accessorial charges not listed above must be approved in writing by SRL dispatch before they are incurred; unapproved charges will not be honored. Carrier shall not re-broker, assign, or subcontract this load, and SRL pays only the carrier named on page 1. Any change to rate, stops, or schedule is valid only when issued by SRL as a revised Rate Confirmation.

If Carrier will miss a pickup or delivery appointment for any reason, Carrier must notify SRL dispatch immediately and before contacting the customer. Where Carrier's late pickup or delivery, missed appointment, or documentation failure directly causes a documented customer chargeback, fine, or penalty, Carrier is liable for that amount, not to exceed the line-haul revenue of this load absent gross negligence. Carrier communicates driver available hours at booking and again when empty; where hours prevent on-time service SRL will adjust the schedule or release Carrier without penalty. The safe and legal operation of the vehicle supersedes any instruction from SRL or its customer.

Requirements
Before dispatchDriver calls SRL dispatch before heading to the shipper. Trailer must be food grade, clean, dry, odor free, fully empty, and used exclusively for this load.
At the shipperDriver verifies load number, PO, and destination on the BOL before leaving. Seal is applied at origin and the seal number recorded on the BOL. Freight secured with a minimum of two load locks or straps. Carrier scales the load before departing and is responsible for legal weight.
In transitMaintain 34°F continuous for the full transit; reefer download required at delivery. Tracking for the duration of the load by any mutually agreed method: ELD integration, third-party platform, the Caravan carrier portal, driver location sharing, or check calls at pickup, in transit, and at delivery. Check-call requests are answered within 30 minutes during business hours. A broken or missing seal must be reported to SRL dispatch before unloading.
InsuranceCarrier keeps its auto liability, cargo, and general liability insurance in force for the full duration of this load and notifies SRL at once of any cancellation or lapse.
CaliforniaAny equipment operating in California must comply with California Air Resources Board regulations, including the Truck and Bus Rule and TRU requirements. Carrier is responsible for resulting fines.
