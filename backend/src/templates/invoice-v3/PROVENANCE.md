## What is NOT here

- **The fill step.** Each delivered packet was produced by writing that invoice's data into the
  `<script id="invoice-data">` block, printing it with `print.cjs`, and merging the BOL and POD
  pages behind it. The script that did the filling and merging was deleted in the 2026-09-25
  cleanup and is not preserved. This folder can re-print an invoice page. It cannot rebuild a
  delivered packet on its own.
- **The delivered packets.** They carry SRL's full bank account number and are not committed.
  Their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `docs/sent-invoices/bkn-2026-09/README.md`, which is the
  record of what was delivered.

## Read before printing anything from this template

- **The Account field in the remit-to block is not the account printed on the delivered
  packets.** It is the value from `docs/design/invoice.html`. Do not send a customer an invoice
  printed from this template without correcting it. The correct account is deliberately not
  written here.
- `README.md` uses example numbers (`SRL-50002`, `INV-20418`) from before the numbering
  correction. Loads now use the bare number (`121498`), and the invoice is that number plus
  `I` (`121498I`). See CLAUDE.md §21.2.
- `print.cjs` loads Playwright from an absolute path in the main checkout
  (`C:/Users/Wasi Haider/dev/silk-route-logistics/node_modules/@playwright/test`). Run it from
  that machine, or change the `require`.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts, so printing needs internet access.
